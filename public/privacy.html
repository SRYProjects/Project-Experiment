PROJECT MEANINGFUL

Privacy Notice

Effective October 11, 2026

Project Meaningful is a public experiment operated by Meaningful Ventures, Inc. This notice explains what information the experiment uses, what becomes public, and the choices participants have about their data.

Information we use

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, public username, and account timestamps.
  • Participant content: Meaningful Actions and Discoveries you submit, their categories, timestamps, and moderation status.
  • Security information: request/network information needed for authentication, Cloudflare Turnstile, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. Project Meaningful stores keyed hashes rather than raw email or IP values in its rate-limit table.
  • Administrative records: if an account is authorized for moderation, administrative actions may be recorded in an audit log.

What is public

When a submission is published, its public username, content, category when applicable, and timestamp are visible to other visitors. Pending and rejected submissions are not public. Your email address and private account identity are not displayed publicly.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate participants, operate Your Record, publish approved contributions, calculate real participation statistics, moderate content, prevent abuse, maintain security, and operate the experiment.

Service providers

Project Meaningful uses service providers necessary to operate the experiment, including Cloudflare for hosting, security, Turnstile, and AI-assisted moderation; Supabase for authentication and database services; and Resend for transactional authentication email. Information is processed by these providers only as needed to provide those services.

Demo content

The experiment may display clearly fictional composite launch examples to make the community experience understandable before there is substantial real participation. Demo content is not tied to real participant accounts and is excluded from real participation statistics.

Data export and deletion

Signed-in participants can download a JSON copy of their Project Meaningful account data from Your Record. Participants can also permanently delete their account. Account deletion removes the authentication account, profile, Meaningful Actions, and Discoveries associated with that account.

If an account has been used for administration, its audit events may be retained after the account identifier is removed so the integrity of the moderation audit trail is preserved. Short-lived hashed abuse-prevention records may remain for approximately two days before automatic or opportunistic cleanup.

Browser storage

The site uses browser storage necessary to maintain authentication sessions and the participation flow. Project Meaningful does not include advertising or social-network tracking features.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the experiment changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Questions about privacy or Project Meaningful can be sent to pr@meaningfulventuresinc.com.
