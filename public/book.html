THE BOOK

Meaningful

The Life You Mean to Live

Most of us can feel the distance between the life that accumulated around us and the life we would deliberately choose. This book examines that gap without selling reinvention, perfection, or a new identity.

Ask about the book on Substack → See the larger vision

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

How much of your life did you actually choose?

The book distinguishes between the incidental life—the life that accumulates through reasonable choices, momentum, habit, and circumstance—and the verasa life, the same life examined and claimed from the inside.

The problem is not that the incidental life is necessarily bad. It is that it keeps moving without requiring a decision, while deliberate living has to be chosen again and again.

01

The incidental life

Family history, available opportunities, careers, relationships, obligations and sensible next steps gradually acquire momentum of their own.

02

The verasa life

Private knowledge, tested values and honest examination become the basis for choosing what to do with the life already in front of you.

THE DAILY PRACTICE

Simple enough to do. Demanding enough to matter.

  1. 1
    Name what is true right now.

    Be specific. Do not hide inside slogans.

  2. 2
    Choose one concrete direction.

    Decide what acting from that truth requires today.

  3. 3
    Do it before the day ends.

    The measure is action, followed by honest review and return.

WHAT IT OFFERS

Not a promise of transformation. A way to own the choices you make from what you know.

Failure is expected. The work is not an unbroken streak; it is the reliability of return. The book is written for people who already feel the gap and want a practice sturdy enough for ordinary life—the full calendar, imperfect relationships and difficult tradeoffs included.
