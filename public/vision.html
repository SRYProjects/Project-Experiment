THE VISION

A platform for deliberate living.

Project Meaningful exists to help people close the gap between the life they are living and the life they actually intend—by making examination practical, action concrete, and return possible after the inevitable failures.

WHAT IT EXISTS TO DO

Turn intention into something usable.

Awareness is rarely the whole problem. People often know the conversations they have deferred, the directions they have not claimed and the habits that keep making decisions for them.

Project Meaningful is designed to make deliberate living operational: a process for examining what matters, a daily practice for acting on what is found, and a community that helps people keep returning.

IDEAL

The process

IDEAL is the structured method for examining what you actually value, identifying the gap between intention and action, and choosing a deliberate direction.

MEANINGFUL

The daily practice

Meaningful sustains the work through small daily acts: name what is true, choose a concrete direction, act, review, and return.

THE ARCHITECTURE

One idea. Several ways to practice it.

The components are intended to reinforce one another rather than become disconnected products.

01

Daily practice application

A deeper tool for morning examination, direction-setting, action and end-of-day accounting.

02

Community

A public and private record of people deliberately acting on what matters and noticing what follows.

03

Books and ideas

The intellectual foundation: why the gap persists, how to examine it and how to return to deliberate action.

04

Courses

Structured learning for individuals, leaders and organizations that want to apply the underlying method.

05

Speaking and gatherings

Conversations, events and eventually gatherings for people who want to examine the work together.

06

Organizational application

Selective work helping organizations apply deliberate examination and action to how they operate.

THE STANDARD

Not perfection. Return.

The incidental life keeps moving without permission. Deliberate living requires repeated choice. The standard is therefore not an unbroken streak; it is whether people can notice that they drifted, recover, and choose again.

START WHERE THE PROJECT IS LIVE

Take one deliberate action.

The public experiment is the simplest expression of the larger vision: act on something that matters, record it, and notice what happens over time.
